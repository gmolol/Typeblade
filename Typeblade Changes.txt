Typeblade Changes
Gerrycho Morales
Individual 2D Project
CS583


Core idea stayed the same: 
You defeat a boss by typing the words in its attacks, which reflects them back at it. Light, heavy, and phrase attacks still deal 1, 3, and 5 damage. The phrase still gives a warning before it fires, and the blade meter still fills +5% / +15% / +25% and spawns "unleash" for 20 damage. 


Gameplay Changes:
* Layout: the design had the boss at the top and the player at the bottom. I decided with a side view where the samurai is on the left, boss/tengu on the right, with words flying across in three lanes. (Easier to implement with the art I found online)
* Boss HP: 100 -> 50, since the second phase refills his health (see below). 
* Second phase: the design said it starts at half health with more frequent attacks. new:
* starts at 20% HP, the first form can't die before transforming
* the crow tengu transforms into its true form (a masked daitengu) with a cutscene: slow motion, flicker, white flash, "TRUE FORM" banner, and a taunt line
* his HP goes back to full, attacks come faster (2.5s -> 1.8s) and words move 25% faster
* words also dive from the top of the screen, straight down or diagonally
* Word lists: instead of picking by length only, the words are themed around the story (tengu, crows, ink, poetry, the red moon). A shuffle bag makes sure every word shows up once before any repeats.
* Same-letter problem (from my Challenges section): I ended up doing both ideas:
* game avoids spawning a word whose first letter is already on screen
* typing locks onto the closest matching word.
* Phrase warning: the boss glows, a "!!!" blinks, and a crow caw plays (a demon growl in phase two with a pity timer so a phrase is guaranteed at least every 20 seconds.
* Unleash: added a cutscene: the world freezes, the screen dims, the camera zooms in on the samurai, and the hit lands as a 4-hit burst instead of one hit.
* Menus: it can now be typed or clicked (type "start",  "options", "retry", etc.), so you never have to leave the keyboard.
* Settings: the music/sfx sliders also work from the pause menu, not just the title screen.


Added features (not in the design):
* Intro cutscene: fade in, title card, the samurai runs in, the tengu speaks with a typewriter effect, and you type "you" to answer him and start the fight.
* Grades: results screen gives an S/A/B/C/F grade in a hanko-style seal, based on accuracy, WPM, and max combo. Losing caps the grade at B.
* High score: best score/best grade are saved and shown on the title screen.
* Results quotes: a haiku when you win, a tengu taunt when you lose.
* Combo counter: pops on each word from x3 and changes color at 10 and 25.
* Low HP warning: a red vignette that pulses like a heartbeat, faster as HP drops.
* features: camera shake on hits, falling petals, a wind slash that carries reflected words, hit flashes, and the moon changing color between phases and fading on victory.
* Talking voice: Undertale-style blips when the tengu speaks.


Art Changes:
- Attacks are styled text instead of three projectile shapes. Reflected words are carried
  by a wind slash effect, and the Unleash has its own impact burst.
- The boss has two full forms (crow and masked true form), each with idle, attack, hurt,
  and death animations. The player has idle, run, parry, unleash, and death animations.
- The HUD shows HP bars, the blade meter, and the combo. Score and accuracy moved to the
  results screen so the fight screen stays clean.


Audio Changes:
- Removed: a sound on every correct letter, tried a few and got annoying fast, so a wrong letter has an error beep and a finished word plays the parry clang.
- Removed: menu click sounds and a sound for the blade meter filling.
- Changed: the "scream when reaching phase two" became an impact plus a demonic laugh.
- Added: crow caw / demon growl phrase warnings, countdown knocks plus a gong on FIGHT!, a layered Unleash sequence (windup ring, drum boom, sword spin, unsheathe, slash), a boss death screech, a player death grunt, and bamboo flute stings for victory and defeat. The fight music fades out when someone dies so the ending sound stands out.
- Music: a calm track on the title screen and a battle track that starts on FIGHT!, kept low so the sound effects are clear.


Stretch Goals not completed:
- Upgrades, powerups, and difficulty levels (listed under Scope creep). I focused on
  making the one-boss version polished instead.
